Outreach Section
# Team Connections
During our Rookie year, Orion | NorthStarRobotics was a mentee team within the Global Mentorship program where we were introduced to two teams. #1574 MisCar and #19176 Broboti. They mentored us in robotic aspects while we helped provide them with outreach opportunities. This next season we are planning to act as a mentor team for business related aspects and cannot wait to expand our connections with the GMP!

<br>

We also connected with another local FTC team #30458 the Masterminds. With this opportunity we helped support them through their portfolio and judging presentation.

<br>

We also hold connections through multiple homeschool co-ops throughout central Indiana where we recruit members from.
